Crokinole privacy policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

App: Crokinole (package id com.VRoadGames.Crokinole) for Meta Quest headsets, and its flat-screen version for Windows PCs and Android phones and tablets.

Who we are: VRoad Games, the trading name of Christos Lougiakis, an individual developer in Athens, Greece ("we").

Contact: christos.lougiakis@gmail.com

The short version

  • Crokinole has no accounts of its own. You do not sign up, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any contact details.
  • There are no ads and no analytics in the app. We do not sell data.
  • Your hand, head and body movements are used on your device to move your hands and your avatar. If you play online, the other players at your table receive your avatar's movements and, unless you mute your microphone, your voice, live. Voice passes through the multiplayer provider's servers and is not recorded.
  • In mixed reality the app reads the layout of your room and, if you allow it, the headset camera, to place and light the board. This happens on your device. No picture of your room is stored or sent.
  • Your settings and progress are stored on your device only.
  • If you report a player, the report is stored on your device only. Nobody receives it yet.
  • Online play runs on services operated by other companies (Normal for multiplayer, Meta for the platform). They process some data to make it work, described below.

What the app processes, and why

Movement data (hands, head, body)

To let you play with your hands, the app reads the positions of your hands, fingers and head from the device. This is used on the device to drive the physics hands, the menus and your avatar. It is not stored.

Your avatar has a full body. On headsets with body tracking, and if you grant the system's body-tracking permission, the app reads the device's estimate of where your body's joints are and uses the positions of your feet to place the avatar's legs. This happens on the device and is not stored. If you refuse the permission, or the device is not sure where your feet are, the app stands the avatar's feet on the floor below you instead.

When you sit at an online table, the pose of your avatar (head, hands, fingers, body and legs, as a compact stream) and the positions of the discs you move are sent through the multiplayer service to the other players at the same table, so that they can see you play. It is sent live and is not kept by us.

The flat-screen version uses a mouse or a touch screen and reads no movement data.

Eye gaze (LOOK control mode only)

If you choose the LOOK control mode on a device with eye tracking, and you grant the system's eye-tracking permission, the app reads the direction of your gaze to aim shots and to select buttons. This happens on the device. The gaze direction is not stored. It is not sent to us or to other players, and the avatar that other players see is not driven by eye or face tracking. On devices without eye tracking, or if you refuse the permission, the app uses the direction of your head. You can leave the LOOK mode at any time. The other control modes do not use eye tracking.

Voice

At online tables, if you grant the microphone permission, your voice is sent live through the multiplayer service to the other players at the same table. The app also works out mouth shapes from your voice, on the device, so that your avatar's mouth moves; they travel with the avatar's movements. We do not record or store voice. You can mute your own microphone, and you can mute any other player, in the settings panel.

Other players can hear what you say, and we cannot control what they do with what they hear.

Tables and table codes

To put players at the same table, the multiplayer service receives a room name, which contains the table code (a five-character code you can share with friends). Anyone who has the code can join that table while it is open. We do not link table codes to your identity. The app remembers the last table code only while it is running, so that you can rejoin.

Avatar and Meta account information

On a Meta Quest headset, the app uses Meta's Platform SDK to check that it is entitled to run for your Meta account and to load your Meta Avatar. For this the app receives from Meta an identifier for your account in this app and an access token for the avatar service. We use them only for these purposes, on the device and in calls to Meta's services. We do not store them on servers of our own.

When you sit at an online table, that identifier is sent through the multiplayer service to the other players at the same table, so that their devices can load your avatar from Meta. The access token is never sent to other players.

If your Meta Avatar is not available, the app gives you one of its preset avatars. The appearance of your avatar and the parlour outfit you choose are shown to the other players at your table.

Your room (mixed reality)

If you turn passthrough on (Meta Quest 3 and 3S), and you grant the system's spatial data permission, the app reads the room layout your headset has stored: the positions and sizes of walls, floor, ceiling, tables and other furniture, as simple shapes. It uses them on the device to stand the board on a real table and to lay out decoration on your walls, floor and tables. The layout is not stored by the app and is not sent to us or to other players. To find the board again where you left it, the app saves a spatial anchor on the headset and keeps its identifier in its settings.

The app also uses the headset's depth sensing, on the device, so that real things and your hands can hide virtual ones. Depth data is not stored or sent.

If you grant the headset camera permission, the app reads small, low-resolution camera frames a few times a second and reduces each at once to a handful of average colours, to light the board like your room. The frames are processed on the device, are never stored and are never sent anywhere. If you refuse, the board is lit with a neutral light.

"Same room" play (shared spatial anchors)

If you use the "Same room" feature, the device creates a spatial anchor, which is a reference point in your physical space, and shares it through Meta's services with the other players who join the same group, so that the board appears in the same place for everyone. This requires Enhanced Spatial Services (point cloud sharing) to be enabled in the device settings, which is a Meta setting governed by Meta's privacy policy. The app itself receives only the anchor's identifier and its position. It does not receive images of your room. The anchor's identifier and the group identifier are sent to the players at your table through the multiplayer service. Players who share a room this way are not played back to each other through the headset. The feature is optional and off until you use it.

Reports

If you report another player, the app writes a record to a file on your device. It contains the time, the table code, the reported player's number at the table, their platform user identifier if the app has one, and the reason you chose. The same line is written to the device's system log. The reported player is also muted for you. If you use the headset's own Report button, Meta is told whether the app handled the report.

Reports are not sent to us or to anyone else, and nobody reviews them at present. If a player's behaviour needs action now, use Meta's own reporting tools as well.

Settings and progress

The app stores your preferences and progress on the device: control mode, handedness, height offset, sound and microphone mute, the game length you chose against the computer, whether you finished the tutorial, the avatar, outfit and styles you chose, what you have unlocked, and your play counts (20s, games, wins, daily goals and stars). This data stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Diagnostic logs

The app writes technical messages to the device's system log (for example connection state and errors). They stay on the device unless you choose to share them with us. The app does not send crash reports or usage statistics to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, payment details, images from the device's cameras, the layout of your room, or recordings of your voice. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Where data goes

The multiplayer service and Meta's services may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under their own terms and privacy policies.

How long data is kept

  • Movement, gaze, voice, room and camera data: not stored by us. It exists only while it is being used or sent.
  • Table state: until the table closes.
  • Reports, settings and progress: on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Data held by Normal or Meta: as their policies say.

Your choices

  • Play offline: the tutorial, the computer opponent and the practice table send nothing to the multiplayer service.
  • Refuse or revoke permissions (microphone, eye tracking, body tracking, spatial data, headset camera) in the device settings. The app keeps working without them, with the related feature off.
  • Mute your microphone, or mute other players, in the settings panel.
  • Use a private table with a code if you do not want to meet strangers.

Deleting your data

We hold no account for you and no personal data on servers of our own. To delete what the app has stored, uninstall it or clear its data in the device settings. To ask about or delete data held by Meta or by the multiplayer provider, use their tools, or write to us at christos.lougiakis@gmail.com and we will help you direct the request. If we come to hold any data about you, write to the same address and we will delete it within 30 days, unless we must keep it by law.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another place with similar law, you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to data portability. Where we rely on your permission (microphone, eye tracking, body tracking, spatial data, headset camera), you can withdraw it at any time in the device settings. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Ours is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (https://www.dpa.gr).

Legal bases: we process movement, voice and table data to provide the game you asked to play (performance of a contract), reports to keep online play safe (legitimate interests), and data behind a device permission on the basis of that permission (consent).

Children

Crokinole's online tables include live voice chat with other players, some of whom you will not know. The app is intended for players aged 13 and over (or the minimum age for a Meta account in your country, if that is higher). It is not directed at younger children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, write to us and we will delete anything we hold.

Security

We keep no accounts and no servers of our own, so we hold nothing that could be exposed. Reports and settings on your device are protected by the device. Data on its way to other players is handled by the multiplayer service and by Meta's services under their own security measures.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle data differently, for example when a moderation service for reports is added, we will update this page and change the effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
